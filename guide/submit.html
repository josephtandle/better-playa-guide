How it works

This guide is crowdsourced. Paste everything at once in the box below: type out a flyer, paste a lineup, drop your whole camp schedule. One block of text, as many events as you like. Or snap a photo of the flyer itself and attach it. It gets read and checked automatically and lands in the guide for everyone, usually within the hour. Needs signal to send, so do it before you roll through the gate or when you catch wifi.

What to include

For each event, as much of this as you have:

  • Event name and what happens there
  • Camp or art car hosting it
  • Address, like 8:15 & E or 3:00 & Esplanade
  • Day and time, like Wednesday 10pm to 2am
  • Who's playing or leading it (DJ names, teachers, speakers)
  • Camp website or Instagram handle, like @yourcamp. This helps verify it's real and gets your camp credited as the source.

Don't worry about format. Write it how a flyer would say it. Missing a detail? Send it anyway.

Paste it all here

No account, no email needed. Submissions are checked automatically; anything that isn't a real playa event gets filtered out. Events show a "Community" badge so people know the source tier.
