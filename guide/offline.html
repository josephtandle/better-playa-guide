How it works

Every event, the map, and search live inside this page. Once you add it to your home screen and open it once with signal, your phone keeps a full copy. After that it needs no bars, no wifi, no data: it is not "loading a website", it is opening the copy already on your phone.

iPhone (Safari)

1

Open playaguide.musecafe.vip in Safari (not Instagram's built-in browser: if you got here from IG, tap ⋯ and choose Open in external browser first).

2

Tap the Share button: the square with the arrow pointing up, bottom-middle of the screen.

3

Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

4

THE STEP EVERYONE MISSES: find the new Playa Guide icon on your home screen and open it from the icon once, right now, while you still have signal. iPhones keep a separate copy for the icon: if you never open the icon online, it has nothing saved and will fail on playa. Open it, scroll for a few seconds, done forever.

Android (Chrome)

1

Open playaguide.musecafe.vip in Chrome (same trick if you're inside Instagram: ⋮ then Open in Chrome).

2

Tap the ⋮ menu, top right.

3

Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app), then confirm.

4

Then open the new icon once while you still have signal and scroll around. The icon keeps its own copy: this step is what makes it work with zero bars.

Prove it to yourself

1

Turn on Airplane Mode.

2

Open the guide from the home screen icon.

3

Search something. If results come up, you're playa-proof. If not, turn airplane mode off, open it once more with signal, and test again.

If it doesn't work

  • Always open from the home screen icon, not from a link in a chat or Instagram: those open a fresh browser that may need signal.
  • The first open ever needs signal. If you added the icon but never opened it, it has nothing saved yet.
  • Private/incognito mode can't save anything. Use a normal tab.
  • "Low data mode" or nearly-full storage can stop the save. Free a little space and open it once more.
  • Events added after you go offline obviously won't appear until you next catch signal (Center Camp wifi works): just open the icon again and it updates itself.
