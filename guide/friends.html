How it works: invite a close friend with a link. When you both accept, you see each other's last known playa address. There is no live GPS out here: locations update whenever either phone catches signal (Center Camp wifi works), so it's a rendezvous tool, not a tracker. Friends stay on your list for next year. Sharing is off until you turn it on, and you can mute anyone, or everyone, any time.

Before anything is shared: turning this on stores your burner name and the playa address you set in the guide, and shows them ONLY to friends you explicitly add. Nothing else, no GPS history, no phone number, no account. Off-playa this all goes dark automatically.
