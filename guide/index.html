      Take it with you

      There is no signal on playa. All the data lives inside this page, so from your home screen it works with no bars, no wifi, no battery drain.

      How to take it offline, step by step

      Short version: iPhone: Share icon, then Add to Home Screen. Android: the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home Screen. Then open the icon once while you still have signal: the icon keeps its own copy, and skipping that open is the #1 reason it fails on playa.

      The buttons below send the link to someone else. They do not install anything on your own phone.
