Reading a playa address

7:30 & E means the spoke at 7:30 on the clock, out at the fifth ring road. The Man sits at the centre. 12:00 points to the top of the map, 6:00 is Center Camp. Rings go Esplanade, Ararat, Bodhi, Ceiba, Delphi, Eternal, Fulcrum, Great Oak, Heiau, Iroko, Jiba, Kundalini: so "E" is Eternal.

Camps often write the street's name rather than its letter: "9:30 & Bodhi" is 9:30 & B.
