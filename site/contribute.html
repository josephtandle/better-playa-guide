Make it better

This is open source and deliberately unfinished. The most useful thing you can give it is a schedule or correction the official guide lacks.

How a submission works

When you click any option below, it creates a pre-filled GitHub issue. Someone folds the submission into the dataset, and the page updates for everyone on the next build.

Ways to contribute

Submit your camp's official schedule or guide

Screenshot, PDF, photo of the whiteboard: whatever you have. Drag it into the issue so it gets read and added, credited to your camp. This is the single highest-value thing anyone can contribute.

Submit our camp's schedule

Submit a camp's Instagram

Most camps publish their real programme on Instagram and nowhere else. Share the handle so it goes on the watch list to be scraped next time.

Report a wrong time or address

Spotted a wrong time, a wrong address, or a camp that moved? Report the fix so we can update it.

Run it yourself

The guide is the friendly face of a recommendation engine that knows your taste. Point it at your own preferences: your camp, how you get around, what you're into, who you want to see, and it ranks the whole programme for you instead of everyone.

git clone https://github.com/josephtandle/better-playa-guide
cd better-playa-guide && npm install
cp -r profile.example profile  # then edit it
node src/index.js now --from "7:30 & E"

It also drops into an AI assistant as an agent: ask it "what's on near me in the next two hours" and it answers from your own preferences. There's a Telegram bot in there too, so you can text it from the playa. Bring your own keys: nothing phones home to us.

What we'd love most

  • Camp schedule images: the single biggest gap. The official listings simply don't have them.
  • Camp Instagram handles: we have 211, there are 1,217 camps.
  • Speaker names: if you know who's actually talking, the official guide usually doesn't say.
  • Corrections: especially addresses. A wrong address sends someone across the city.
